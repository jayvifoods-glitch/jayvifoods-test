JAYVI FOODS — V31.0 WEBSITE RELEASE

V31.0 is a refinement pass on the V30.0 redesign: fixes the mobile cart
quantity control layout, adds distinct background bands between sections,
replaces the see-through hamburger menu with a solid dark drawer, and
gives combo cards a multi-image swipeable gallery. See DEPLOY.md for full
details and the Admin login credentials.
